Amaatra Aero Heights Apartments – Your Gateway to Luxury Living in Noida Extension

Amaatra Aero Heights is designed to offer a lifestyle that feels modern, spacious, and future-oriented. Here’s what makes it special:

✔ Premium Location in Noida Extension

Located in one of the fastest-growing corridors of NCR—Greater Noida West (Noida Extension)—the project offers:

  • Upcoming metro expansion adding major convenience

  • Near educational institutes, markets, hospitals, and corporate hubs

This makes the project ideal for working professionals and families seeking a well-connected neighbourhood.

Amaatra Aero Heights apartments are crafted with attention to natural light, airflow, and intelligent layout. They offer:

  • 2 BHK & 3 BHK configurations

  • Large balconies with scenic views

  • Vaastu-friendly design

  • Premium flooring, fittings, and modular features

Every flat is designed to maximize space utilization while ensuring privacy and comfort.

  • Landscaped gardens

  • Children’s play area

  • Walking & jogging tracks

  • 24×7 security with CCTV surveillance

  • Ample parking space

  • Power backup & high-speed elevators

The amenities make this project not just a home, but a complete lifestyle destination.

The project offers premium features at budget-friendly pricing, making it perfect for middle-class buyers seeking luxury at affordable rates.

✔ High Rental Demand

Proximity to employment zones, colleges, and commercial hubs ensures strong rental income opportunities.

Amaatra Aero Heights is well-connected to major landmarks:

  • 5–10 minutes from Sector 78 & Sector 76 Metro Stations
